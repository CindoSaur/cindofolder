ABOUT ME

hey, i'm Cindo — a computer science student at FPT Polytechnic and a self-taught developer who enjoys turning ideas into websites, games, and creative digital experiences.

when i'm away from the keyboard, you'll probably find me drawing, writing haiku, or getting lost in manga and light novels. i also know a little japanese — mostly because it makes my haiku sound cooler than they actually are.

i love building things: web apps, Unity games, pixel-art interfaces, and little experiments that help me learn something new. every project is a chance to improve, explore, and have a little fun along the way.

this portfolio is my little corner of the internet — a place where i share what i've built, what i'm learning, and the ideas i'm excited to bring to life next. have a look around, and if you'd like to chat, collaborate, or just say hi, my inbox is always open :)
